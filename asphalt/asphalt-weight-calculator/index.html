Quick answer

How much does a cubic yard of asphalt weigh?

A cubic yard of compacted hot mix asphalt weighs about 3,915 lb (1.96 US tons), based on a density of 145 lb/ft³. The same yard loose in a truck weighs about 3,155 lb, and compacted millings about 3,290 lb, so always confirm which state a quote or ticket refers to.

Rule of thumb:
1 compacted cubic yard of asphalt ≈ 2 tons.
Formula:
Weight (lb) = Volume (ft³) × 145

Weight = volume × density

Asphalt weight is volume times density, and the skill is choosing the density that matches the material's state. There's only one formula on this page. Asphalt exists on your job in at least three states (loose in the truck, compacted in the pavement, broken in the demo pile), and each has its own unit weight. Full numbers and the reasons behind them live in the asphalt density chart and the weight chart. Comparing against cement-based materials? The concrete density chart is the companion page.

Loose vs compacted: the 25% trap

Same yard, different weight

Quotes, tickets and specs silently switch between states. A supplier selling millings "by the yard" means loose yards off the pile (103 lb/ft³); your pavement design needs compacted yards (122). Buy loose volume for a compacted requirement and you arrive 16% short. Convert to tons first, because weight never lies.

Sanity-check any delivery

Divide the scale ticket's net tons by the truck's struck volume. Hot mix should land near 1.55–1.6 tons per loose cubic yard. A number far off means the wrong material or a wrong ticket, so catch it before the truck dumps.

Frequently asked questions

What density does the asphalt weight calculator use?
The calculator uses 145 lb/ft³ for compacted hot mix, about 117 lb/ft³ for loose hot mix, 137 lb/ft³ for compacted cold mix, and 103 lb/ft³ loose or 122 lb/ft³ compacted for millings. Pick the state that matches what you measured; the 25% gap between loose and compacted hot mix causes more ordering errors than any other figure in paving.
How much does a 3-inch asphalt driveway weigh per square foot?
A 3-inch asphalt driveway weighs about 36 lb per square foot, at 12 lb per inch of compacted thickness (145 ÷ 12), which is handy for estimating removal weight on tear-outs.
How many truckloads does an asphalt tear-out need?
Divide the in-place tonnage by the truck's legal payload: about 13–15 tons for a tandem, 16–19 for a tri-axle and 23–25 for an end dump. A 24-ton tear-out is two tandem loads. The truck load capacity guide has the full payload table.
How much does removed (demolition) asphalt weigh?
Removed asphalt weighs the same as it did in place: area × thickness × 145 lb/ft³. Loaded in the truck as broken chunks it bulks enormously, so plan on nearly double the truck volume. Disposal is priced by the ton, so the in-place number is the one your budget needs.
Does temperature change how much asphalt weighs?
Only trivially. A ton of mix weighs the same hot or cooled; what changes density is mix design and compaction. Compacted hot mix reaches 145 lb/ft³ while cold mix stays near 137 lb/ft³ because it cannot be rolled as tight, as covered in hot mix vs cold mix weight.
