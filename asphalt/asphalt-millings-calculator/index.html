Quick answer

How many tons of asphalt millings do I need?

Multiply length × width × compacted depth in feet, then by 122 lb/ft³ (rolled millings), and divide by 2,000 for tons. A 12 × 50 ft driveway at 4 inches compacted needs 12.2 tons, or about 13.4 tons with 10% waste, which is roughly 9.7 loose cubic yards.

Rule of thumb:
About 2 tons of millings per 100 ft² at 4 inches compacted.
Formula:
Tons = Area × depth × 122 ÷ 2,000 × (1 + waste)

Loose vs compacted millings

Weight stays the same when millings are rolled; volume shrinks.

A loose cubic yard of millings off the stockpile weighs about 2,780 lb. Rolled in place, the same material packs to about 3,290 lb per yard. That is why this calculator works from the compacted depth you want and converts back to loose yards only for ordering. Densities for every asphalt state, including hot mix and cold patch, are in the asphalt density chart.

Buying by the yard? Ask which yard

A supplier quoting "10 yards" almost always means loose yards. If you calculated 10 compacted yards and order 10 loose, you arrive about 16% short. Order in tons when you can; a scale ticket removes the ambiguity.

Base first on soft ground

Millings bind slightly as the old binder warms and re-sets, but they cannot bridge soft clay. On poorly drained soil, place 4 to 6 inches of compacted crushed stone first; size it with the driveway gravel calculator.

Millings questions

How many tons of millings do I need for a driveway?
About 2 tons per 100 ft² at 4 inches compacted. A 12 × 50 ft driveway at 4 inches needs 12.2 tons before waste, or about 13.4 tons with 10%, which is one tandem dump truck load.
How much does a cubic yard of asphalt millings weigh?
About 2,780 lb (1.39 tons) loose, as delivered off the pile, and about 3,290 lb (1.65 tons) once rolled in place. The loose figure is the one to use when a supplier sells millings by the yard.
How much do millings compact?
Roughly 16%. Loose millings run about 103 lb/ft³ and rolled millings about 122 lb/ft³, so one loose yard compacts to about 0.84 yd³. Spread about 4.75 inches loose to finish at 4 inches after rolling.
How deep should asphalt millings be?
3 to 4 inches compacted for a residential driveway over a firm, well-drained base, and 6 inches on soft clay or for farm lanes that carry heavy equipment. Place deep lifts in two passes and roll each one.
Are millings the same as crushed asphalt?
Yes for estimating purposes. Millings are ground off an existing road by a milling machine; crushed asphalt is broken slab run through a crusher. Both are recycled asphalt pavement (RAP), and both use the same loose and compacted densities in this calculator.
Do millings need a gravel base?
On sandy, well-drained soil they can go straight onto compacted subgrade. On clay or wet ground, put 4 to 6 inches of compacted crushed stone underneath first or the millings will rut. The driveway gravel calculator sizes that base layer.
